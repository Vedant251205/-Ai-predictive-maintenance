How priorities are assigned

Priority is derived from the risk band the model puts the asset in, then escalated when more than one parameter has breached a hard limit.

Code Label Triggered when Response window Man-hrs
P1 Emergency Critical band and failure probability above 80%, or two or more hard limits breached Immediate 6.0
P2 Urgent Critical band, or warning band with a hard limit breach Within 24 hours 4.0
P3 High Warning band, no hard limit breached Within 7 days 2.5
P4 Scheduled Good band with elevated risk above 30% Next shutdown window 1.5
P5 Preventive Excellent or Good band, nothing flagged Routine round 0.5
